How We Refute Claims: Automatic Fact-Checking through 
Flaw Identification and Explanation (WWW 2024)
6
https://youtu.be/hLgLXMZczYo
- Existing fact-checking lacks flaw 
identification and deep explanation for 
false claims
- We propose RefuteClaim framework 
defining 7 flaw types with aspect and 
justification generation
- Our model performs well in classifying 
and explaining false claims; best with all 
flaws considered

Educational Lecture Abstract-based Question Generation System 
(IJCAI 2024 Demo)
- The need for anticipating student questions helps reduce lecture preparation effort
- The E-QGen system uses lecture abstracts with multitask LoRA-tuned LLMs to generate student-like question
- E-QGen achieves higher similarity and diversity than GPT-4
8
https://youtu.be/SuiroLobtEU

Vocabulary Test Distractor Generation (ACML 2025)
- Distractor design is time-consuming and 
existing methods generate invalid options
- ISSR uses LLM iterative selection with 
self-review to avoid multiple correct 
answers
- Self-review effectively filters invalid 
options, challenges advanced learners
9
https://youtu.be/u9apI9MoHF8

Refining Financial Consumer Complaints
- Financial consumer complaints often lack 
legal formality and persuasiveness
- The MSMI framework combines a 
classifier and LLMs for iterative legal text 
refinement
- MSMI significantly outperforms 
single-pass generation in success rate and 
robustness

cost-prohibitive, motivating methods that balance accuracy and computational cost

FAQ
問題1：實驗室需要值班嗎？
答：不需要，但是實驗室提供每位同學固定座位。
問題2：實驗室有國科會或是業界的計畫嗎？
答：實驗室有正在執行的國科會計畫與產學合作計畫。同學的研究津貼也從這些計劃提供。
問題3：需要自己找研究題目，還是老師會指定?
答：我會提供研究主題，如果同學有自己感興趣的研究題目，也可以和我討論。

問題4：實驗室是否有固定的seminar或個人meeting?
答：每週安排一次個人meeting，跟我討論研究進度，會議以實體方式進行為主。另外，每週也安排一次seminar，由同學
