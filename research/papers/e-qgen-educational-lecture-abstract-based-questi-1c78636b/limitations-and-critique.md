# Limitations and critique

## Author limitation

The work currently focuses on computer-science courses; the authors propose extending it across fields (p. 3).

## Builder interpretation

The silver alignment labels and platinum GPT-4 augmentation expand a small gold set, but create a quality boundary: some training labels are inferred rather than directly observed. The report also uses best-candidate scoring across 20 generated questions, so it should not be read as the expected quality of a randomly selected output.

## Unresolved question

How would a teacher-facing evaluation measure novelty, appropriateness, and preparation value without rewarding questions that merely echo the lecture abstract?
