# Limitations and critique

## Author limitation

The authors report that combining the LLM and BERT requires more computation than similar work, and that individual binary-choice validations make generation slow (p. 26).

## Builder interpretation

The self-review test asks the same class of model that selected candidates to act as a validity filter. This is a practical heuristic, but correlated failure modes could survive both stages. The evaluation is also tied to Taiwanese GSAT vocabulary items.

## Unresolved question

How do teacher judgments and real learner error patterns change when ISSR distractors are used in new exams?
