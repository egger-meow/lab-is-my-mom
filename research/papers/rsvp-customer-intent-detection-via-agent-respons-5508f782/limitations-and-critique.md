# Limitations and critique

## Author limitation

The paper notes that agent responses cannot be used directly at real-time intent-detection inference; they are training-time metadata (p. 2).

## Builder interpretation

Historical agent responses can encode organizational habits and mistakes. An offline gain should be paired with audits for response quality, privacy, and intents that evolve after deployment.

## Unresolved question

Would the objectives remain helpful with multilingual agents or sparse, one-line responses?
