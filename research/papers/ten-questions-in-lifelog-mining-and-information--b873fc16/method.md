# Method

## Paper claim

This position paper proposes an agenda for mining heterogeneous lifelogs into a personal knowledge base and using it for reactive or proactive information recall (pp. 1-2, 6). It identifies ten questions spanning event extraction, multimodal data, entity linking, retrieval timing, privacy, and ethics.

## Builder interpretation

This is the conceptual spine of the lab's later LED, SEEN, CIVIL, and personal-KB QA work.

## Unresolved question

How can personal knowledge be useful while remaining local, consented, and correctable?
