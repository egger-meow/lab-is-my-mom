# Experiments and results

## Observed result (reported; not reproduced)

On 1,605 MathQA questions, GPT-3.5 reports 66.54% zero-shot accuracy, 65.67% few-shot accuracy, and 66.11% chain-of-thought accuracy (Table 1, p. 2). The paper reports that GPT-3.5 can be misled by human answers and may incorrectly repair valid reasoning (p. 9).

## Evidence caveat

The study is a position/pilot paper using GPT-3.5 and a small human evaluation, not a broad classroom deployment.
