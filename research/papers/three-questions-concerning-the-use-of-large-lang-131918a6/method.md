# Method

## Paper claim

This position paper asks three questions about using GPT-3.5 for adaptive mathematics feedback: how LLMs can help, whether they solve and explain math correctly, and whether they can understand and correct student processes (pp. 1-2).

## Builder interpretation

It establishes the problem framing later operationalized by MathEDU: fluent correction is not trustworthy if the system misunderstands a valid student strategy.

## Unresolved question

Which feedback evaluation best predicts learning rather than answer agreement?
