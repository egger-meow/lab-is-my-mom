# Experiments and results

## Observed result

The paper reports that its personal-KB QA-with-correction system is effective at correcting unanswerable questions (abstract, p. 1). It uses BERT for event extraction/alignment and GraphSAGE features in query-graph scoring, then GenTagNet for construction plus editing (pp. 4-6).

## Evidence caveat

Consult the extracted tables for exact scores; this summary does not treat reported effectiveness as a reproduced result.
