# Limitations and critique

## Author limitation

The paper identifies contextual-image, first-person viewpoint, event-detection, object-hallucination, and label-interpretation errors. Merged captions can propagate an early summary error; GPT-4o reranking is costly (pp. 5–6).

## Builder interpretation

The architecture makes captions auditable but also turns a private-memory retrieval system into one that must handle sensitive visual descriptions carefully.

## Unresolved question

Does performance persist for other lifeloggers rather than the single evaluated NTCIR user?
