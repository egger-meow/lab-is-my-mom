# Experiments and results

## Observed result (reported; not reproduced)

The paper reports state-of-the-art FEB benchmark improvements of 3.4%-5.1% task accuracy and 3.0%-5.8% on its explanation metric for small models (p. 2). It uses models in the 200M-2.7B range and validates the plausibility approximator with human and quantitative evaluations (pp. 1-2).

## Evidence caveat

The result depends on the task-specific NLI mapping and pseudo-label selection; it is not a guarantee that a plausible explanation is correct.
